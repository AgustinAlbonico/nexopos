# Wave 3 — Diferenciadores calibrados

## Por familia
- Unit simple: baseline; no true vertical differentiator.
- FMCG: replenishment + shelf-life/lot only where applicable; barcode/fast checkout are core, not differentiators.
- Weight: UOM/tare/PLU/scale and mixed-unit handling.
- Variants: matrix operations at scale and per-variant identity/stock.
- Lot/expiry: traceability, FEFO and recall, not generic date fields.
- Serial/warranty: unit lifecycle and original-sale linkage.
- Consignment: ownership accounting and settlement.
- Wholesale hybrid: customer pricing/catalog/terms and quantity/UOM rules.

## Mezclas
- Fresh store: FMCG + weight + lot/expiry.
- Electronics reseller: serial/warranty + variants + wholesale.
- Fashion wholesaler: variants + wholesale.
- Resale: consignment + unit + variants.
