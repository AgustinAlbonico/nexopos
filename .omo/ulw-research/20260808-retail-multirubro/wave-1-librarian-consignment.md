# Wave 1 — Consignación y reventa

## Hallazgos
- Estructural: consignante/contrato, propiedad de item, split/comisión, aging/markdown, ledger de liquidaciones y auditoría.
- Esenciales: intake, payout cutoff/hold/reversal, devoluciones al dueño, vencimiento de contrato y reportes por consignante.
- Opcionales: portal, ACH/check/cash/store-credit rails, integraciones, booth rent y multi-location.
- Stock consignado debe separarse contable y operacionalmente del stock propio.

## Fuentes
- Ricochet, SimpleConsign, Lightspeed consignment official docs.
- QuickBooks inventory/consignment guidance.
- CDTFA, Washington DOR and Connecticut consignment tax guidance.

## EXPAND
- none — intake, ownership, splits, markdowns, payouts, returns and reporting saturated.

## CLAIMS
- Consignment is not normal supplier inventory and needs ownership-aware ledgers.
